# PM Agent 决策与循环记录模板 v1.0

> 用于审计 Agent 是否真正经历需求/研究/用户/文案/方案的多轮思考，而不是一次性生成“很好”的总结。可简化记录，但不得缺少至少一项真实的反驳/修订证据。

## Iteration log
| Round | Node D/R/U/C/S | Decision question | V1 assertion/output | Counterargument / evidence | Revised action & V2 difference | Remaining unknown | Result / next route |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Result:
- REVISIT：继续该节点并改变假设/引入新证据
- PASS_WITH_ASSUMPTIONS：暂时可转下一节点，但不等于实证验证
- EVIDENCE_BLOCKED：缺少可得证据，清楚标注并提出成本最低的获取方式
- NEED_USER_DECISION：出现真正需要用户作价值判断的冲突

## Final PM Decision Packet
1. One-sentence **problem** plus real target audience.
2. Why this product/message is relevant and credible.
3. Recommended **strategy**, and why the strongest alternative lost.
4. Module table / source-of-truth copy / CTA destinations.
5. Evidence ledger: verified facts / observations / hypotheses / missing.
6. Three plausible user paths with failure fixes.
7. Remaining uncertainties and what would reverse this recommendation.
8. Next step for Design/Dev or experiment, including explicit not-ready conditions.

## Anti-fake-review checks
- Did review actually change a sentence, hypothesis, module, order, or decision?
- Is a counterexample or a rival interpretation recorded?
- Does the user-simulation clearly say “hypothesis, not observed behavior”?
- Do supporting facts include date and context?
- Is the final packet shorter and clearer than the sum of working notes?
- Have any material unknowns been concealed to make work appear complete?
